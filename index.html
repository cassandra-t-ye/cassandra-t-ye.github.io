Cassandra T. Ye

Hi, I'm Cassandra, and I'm currently a 2nd year Computer Science Ph.D student at Cornell University where I'm advised by Kristina Monakhova. I'm broadly interested in quantifying uncertainty in computational imaging methods for scientific applications.

In a past life, I was an undergraduate at MIT studying Computer Science and Engineering. During my time there, I worked at the Computational Biophotonics Lab headed by Sixian You, working on optimizing computational algorithms for microscopy image reconstruction

You can find my cv here and if you're interested in collaborating or have any questions about my work at all, please don't hesitate to reach out at cassye@cs.cornell.edu!
